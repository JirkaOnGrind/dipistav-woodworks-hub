# Phase 2 blank geometry — State 2 Candidate A v18

## Reference roles

- The approved blank upright Unit Tile is the sole pixel source for both bags.
- The supplied master grid is spatial-layout reference only: two upright bags side-by-side. Its captions and printed surfaces are ignored.
- The approved Phase 1 logo is an exclusion reference and is not composited.

## Deterministic composition

No generative edit is used for State 2. Two byte-identical `520 × 863 px` crops of the approved blank upright Unit Tile are composited without rotation, resampling, perspective changes, or scale changes. They are parallel and separated by a clean `16 px` gap on the standard `1536 × 1024` transparent canvas. Both instances remain at exactly 100% of the State 1 scale anchor.

All visible surfaces remain blank kraft paper. No logo, emblem, text, symbols, pseudo-text, stamps, watermarks, floor, backdrop, cast shadow, or extra objects are added.
