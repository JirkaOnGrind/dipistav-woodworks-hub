# Product-family geometry rules

## Beams

- Use physical front profile `height:width = 1:1`; the cross-section is square.
- Use square or catalog-declared rectangular prisms with congruent projected cross-sections.
- For `3–4`, draw three units in a stable 2+1 stack.
- For `16+`, draw sixteen units in a 4×4 matrix, four top surfaces, and three continuous top seams.

## Planks

- Use physical front profile `height:width = 1:2.3–2.5`; the section must remain massive rather than matchstick-thin.
- Keep thickness constant within a family and use straight, clean, waney-free edges unless the catalog explicitly says otherwise.
- A seam must continue across every visible shared boundary; do not merge adjacent broad faces.
- V11 plank geometry uses `column=(280,-58)`, `rowDown=(0,119)`, and `back=(-372,-183)` design pixels. Its projected width:height ratio is `2.403:1` (height:width `1:2.403`, mandatory range `1:2.3–2.5`) and compact depth is `1.450×` projected width.
- Use seven bands with representative counts `1/2/3/6/9/12/16`: `1`, `2`, `3–4`, `5–8`, `9–11`, `12–15`, `16+`.
- Required layouts are `1×1`, `2×1`, centered `2+1`, `3×2`, `3×3`, `4×3`, and `4×4`.

## Boards

- Use physical front profile `height:width = 1:4.5–5.0`; boards are thinner and broader than planks.
- Keep boards clean-edged and bark-free.
- Narrow family widths: 8, 10, 12, and 14 cm. Wide family widths: 16, 18, and 20 cm. Vary width only when the scene manifest requests it.
- Use the seven v11 bands with representative counts `1/2/3/6/9/12/16` and layouts `1×1`, `2×1`, centered `2+1`, `3×2`, `3×3`, `4×3`, and `4×4`.

## Battens

- Use physical front profile `height:width = 1:1.25–1.5`; battens are small rectangular sections, never square beams or thin boards.
- Keep one rectangular cross-section across a scene.
- Use the seven v11 bands with representative counts `1/2/3/6/9/12/16` and layouts `1×1`, `2×1`, centered `2+1`, `3×2`, `3×3`, `4×3`, and `4×4`.

## Pellet bags and pallets

- Keep every bag or pallet at the same projected scale; depth changes placement and overlap only.
- Preserve the approved tree mark and `15 kg`; reject every other generated word or logo.
- Interlock bags physically and keep individual silhouettes readable.

## Firewood and palletized wood

- Use split wedges with bark only on the outer rounded edge; reject round logs and peeled rectangular blocks.
- Keep cut faces aimed right/down and preserve real physical support.
