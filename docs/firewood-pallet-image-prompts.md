# Firewood pallet illustration prompts

Use the same seed, camera, palette, line weight, light direction, crate design, and 4:3 framing for all four outputs. Render at 1600 × 1200 px. These prompts intentionally repeat the physical constraints so each image can be generated independently without losing the product-family rules.

## 33 cm / 1 prm

Create a polished isometric vector illustration for a professional Central European firewood e-shop: one wooden pallet crate containing exactly 1 prm of cleanly split hardwood logs, each log 33 cm long. Three-quarter isometric camera, front face dominant and right side face visible, clean confident outlines, warm honey, amber, chestnut and muted forest-brown colors, soft restrained shading, subtle grounding shadow, uniform light beige background (#F5EFE3), no text, no labels, no people, no surrounding props. Use a compact, balanced 4:3 product composition with generous clear margin.

PHYSICAL ORIENTATION IS MANDATORY: every log is parallel and stacked in one single longitudinal direction from the rear of the crate toward the front. The front opening/face shows exclusively the freshly sawn cut ends and growth rings of the split logs. The right side opening/face shows exclusively the long split faces and bark running horizontally into depth. Absolutely zero cut ends, circular ends, rings, or end-grain shapes may appear on the right side panel. The visible top layer must confirm the same single direction; no cross-stacking and no randomly rotated pieces.

The firewood must be cleanly split hardwood with sharp wedge-like or irregular polygonal cut ends and crisp split edges. Every visible longitudinal surface must retain natural dark chestnut bark; no pale peeled, debarked, or naked long faces. The pieces must still read as split wedges rather than round trunks. No perfectly round logs, no unsplit branches, no smooth cylindrical poles. Keep log thickness consistent with the other variants. Make the 33 cm depth clearly readable on the visible side through longer bark-covered surfaces. The 1 prm crate should have a moderately low, dense, tidy silhouette, approximately four horizontal crate rails, clearly smaller than the 1.6 prm version while sharing its footprint and visual center.

Negative constraints: side-panel cut ends, side-panel growth rings, mixed log directions, crosswise stack, round logs, branches, pale peeled long faces, debarked logs, smooth cylinders, loose pile, gaps that expose impossible geometry, warped crate, photorealism, 3D render, watercolor, heavy black shadows, orange color cast, busy background, typography, badges, measurements, cropped pallet feet.
